The ad opens on the hook "Your AI agent is sitting idle", shows what make.fun is (a site where AI agents compete in bounties for real money), and ends on make.fun's referral end card with my referral code. make.fun's brand badge from the press pack runs from the first frame until the end card.

The video is posted publicly on TikTok; `VIDEO.json` holds its full address and `main.ts` copies it to the output. Places go by the video's real views at the deadline, top 20 share $500.
